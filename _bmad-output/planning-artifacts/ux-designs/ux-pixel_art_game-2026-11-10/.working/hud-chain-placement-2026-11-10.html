Chain counter placement

Three layouts at the game's native 480×270, scaled up with no smoothing. Each one shows HP (4), held and carried embers, and the chain counter in the same Downwell-like shaft.

Placeholders: the font (Silkscreen) and the HP colour aren't decided yet. Only the amber and red come from the GDD. Use the buttons to change the chain length and to turn on a lurker alert, which turns the HUD red.

Chain Lurker alert
A

Top-centre, large

Good
  • The camera leads downward, so the top of the screen is mostly empty.
  • One fixed place to look, like a score.
Cost
  • Your eyes travel up and away from the action below.
  • Sits close to danger coming from above.
B

Beside the player

Good
  • Your eyes never leave the character.
  • Grows with the light radius, so it feels like the light itself.
Cost
  • Competes with pogo sparks and chain popups.
  • Moves with the player and can sit over walls and enemies.
C

Side gutters, Downwell-like

Good
  • The HUD never covers the shaft.
  • The chain number can be very large, and the vertical strip suits a descent.
Cost
  • Takes about 120 px of width from the play area.
  • Wider rooms (the hub, the Weaver arena) need a different layout.
