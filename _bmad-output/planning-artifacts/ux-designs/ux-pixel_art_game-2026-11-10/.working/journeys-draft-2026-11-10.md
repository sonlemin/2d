# Journey drafts (persona: Son, the developer as playtester)

Son plays on a laptop keyboard, in the web build first and later the PC build.

## J1 — First 5 minutes
1. Son opens the itch page. The title screen shows the slowly scrolling shaft, and the menu lights up under the lantern glow.
2. Son picks New Game, then Normal on the difficulty step. The step says it only changes what death costs and can be changed later.
3. Son drops into the first shaft. Glyph prompts teach jump, slash and pogo, and the right-gutter chain meter starts filling.
4. Son lands safely at chain 6. The chain banks: embers move from held to carried in the left gutter with a popup and a chord.
5. Son reaches the first Lift Station, presses ↓, rests and watches the embers count into the bank. The autosave icon appears.
6. **Climax:** the station menu offers Fast travel. Son rides up to the hub, sees the Cartographer and the Field Journal, and realises there is a whole world.
7. Son opens pause and sees the bank summary and the Map tab. *(The Map tab is E4 and the hub is E5. In M2 builds before those land, J1 ends at step 5.)*

## J2 — Greedy chain, near death
1. Son is at chain 15. The light is wide, the music is in full theme, and the chain number is huge in the right gutter.
2. A faint red glow appears at the left screen edge (a lurker stirring) along with a panned growl. With visual sound cues on, an edge marker shows too.
3. Son clings to a wall to wait. The light drops, the cue by the player shrinks and the meter starts draining from the top.
4. The lurker telegraphs: its eyes open and the HUD turns red for 0.9 s.
5. **Climax:** Son taps V for Flare Toss. The notch at segment 2 says it's allowed. The meter's lower half tints, half the chain goes, and the flare draws the lurker while dimming over 4 s.
6. Son pogoes down to a ledge. The chain banks, embers move to carried, and the music resolves on a chord.

## J3 — Death and cache run
1. Son is holding +30 embers and carrying 40. A crawler hit and then a lurker bite take the last HP.
2. Short fade. The death line reads, for example, "Lurker. It waits where the light is dim. Watch for the red glow at the screen edge." Below it: held embers lost, 40 carried dropped as an Ember Cache. It shows for about 1.5 s, and any key skips it.
3. Son respawns at the last lit anchor with full HP.
4. Son goes back down. The Cache glows amber in the world, and that glow has stirred nearby lurkers, whose red edge glow shows they are waiting around it.
5. **Climax:** Son builds a chain for light and timing, then dives for the Cache. One touch restores the 40 to carried, and the lurkers start to hunt.
6. If Son dies again before reaching it, the Cache is gone. The death line says so.

## J4 — Station → hub → spend
1. Son rests at a Lift Station: full heal, embers deposited, autosave.
2. Son picks Fast travel → Hub from the station menu.
3. Son talks to the Smith. The speech box appears at the bottom, and the shop list shows the deposited total and the prices.
4. The Naturalist pays for new journal entries, and the Journal tab marks them as new.
5. **Climax:** Son checks the Guild records board and sees that their best chain was beaten on this run.
6. Son descends again from the station menu.
