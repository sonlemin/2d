Station Rest and Menu

Key screens for the Lift Station, E1 placeholder through E4 full, Dusk palette (S1, Lip Station). Each frame is the 960×540 game window: 480×270 native at ×2. The station menu is in play, so it takes the active zone (Dusk here). The spines are the contract; these mocks illustrate. Items tagged [ASSUMPTION] are placements the spines do not give.

(a) Rest

One press of ↓ at the station: HP refilled to 4/4, carried embers fly from the player into the bank, which counts up (mid-count: carried 40 → 16, deposited 312 → 336), and the autosave lantern shows at the bottom of the left gutter. No menu until the rest ends.

Spine sections

  • EXPERIENCE State Patterns: Rest, Autosave; Interaction Primitives: One-press rest; Game Feel: Rest; Component Patterns: Ember counter, Autosave icon (≥ 1000 ms, steady)
  • DESIGN Components: Autosave icon (lantern, HP colour, x 30, y 252), Ember counter, HP pip, Text outline; Shapes: lantern, ember
  • Key Flows J1 step 5, J4 step 1

[ASSUMPTION] placed here, not in the spines

  • Where the bank count-up shows: the spines say embers fly "into the bank" but give no on-screen place. Drawn as the Deposited row at the top of the play area (label x 80, value right at x 400, top y 40), with the text outline, so the total stays in the same place when the menu replaces the play area.
  • Ember fly-in path (player → Deposited value) and the carried row counting down in the gutter at the same time.
  • The glyph prompt is hidden during the rest (not specified).
  • Station, cable and player art are world placeholders, not a spec.

(b) Station menu

After the rest: the menu replaces the play area only (surface fill), the gutters stay so the healed HP shows. Deposited total on top; Descend / Fast travel / Leave; ember cursor on Descend. Back = Leave.

Spine sections

  • DESIGN Components: Station menu (D14), Menu item, Ember cursor, Bank summary, Menu screen; Layout & Spacing: menu-row-pitch 24
  • EXPERIENCE Component Patterns: Station menu; IA screen map; Key Flows J1 step 6, J4 steps 2 and 6

[ASSUMPTION] placed here, not in the spines

  • Menu text at x 80, not the menu-text-x token (64). At x 64 the ember cursor (9 px gap, 5 px wide) would sit at x 50, inside the left gutter. x 80 puts the cursor at x 66, inside the play area. Spec conflict to resolve.
  • Deposited row top y 40; items at y 64 / 88 / 112 (the Pause tab item rows); no divider between the total and the items.
  • Cursor starts on Descend.
  • Autosave lantern gone once the save finishes (shown ≥ 1000 ms, then hides).
  • No heading (no station name), no menu-key hint footer: neither is specified.

(c) Fast travel

Fast travel from Lip Station: the hub and the other repaired stations, replacing the items in place. M2 Layer 1 has no broken station, so every lit Layer 1 station is listed; the landing-arena station joins after the Weaver.

Spine sections

  • GDD Lift Station; Economy: Lift Station repairs (Layer 2 onward)
  • Key Flows J4 step 2 ("Fast travel → Hub")
