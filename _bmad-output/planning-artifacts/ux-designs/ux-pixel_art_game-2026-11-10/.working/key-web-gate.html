Web Click-to-Play Gate

Key screens for the web build (itch.io), E1, Dusk palette (menus outside play). Frame (a) is the 960×540 embed: 480×270 native at ×2. Frame (b) is an 800×450 browser window, too small for ×2, so the game runs at ×1 and letterboxes. The spines are the contract; these mocks illustrate. Items tagged [ASSUMPTION] are placements the spines do not give.

(a) Gate at ×2

The 960×540 itch embed on load: one body line in the HP colour on the Dusk surface. The first click (or key) gives focus and unlocks audio, then the title shows.
