Lantern menu directions

Three ways to show "dark screens lit by a lantern glow", all drawn on the pause menu in the cold-violet zone. Each one has the bank summary, the tabs and the menu items you picked.

Use ↑ and ↓ (click the page first) or the buttons to move the selection. The selection eases over about 120 ms; with reduced motion it jumps.

Selection
A

Lantern halo

A soft amber pool of light sits behind the selected item and glides to the next one. Other items stay dim in the zone's colours. It reads as you holding the lantern up to the menu.

B

Ember cursor

A small flickering ember marks the selected item, and only that item's text turns bright. There's no glow, so amber stays tiny on the screen. Closest to Celeste.

C

Light band

A horizontal band of warm light, like a lantern seen through a slot, lights the selected row from edge to edge. Strong and readable, but the most amber on screen.
