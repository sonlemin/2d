In BMad facilitation (game brief, coaching flows), the user asked twice for "options to choose" instead of answering open-ended questions. Asked again 2026-11-10 in gds-ux, when the skill's "brain dump" step was given as open prompts. This applies even when a skill says to collect an open brain dump.

**Why:** Faster for them; they'd rather react to concrete options than write from scratch.

**How to apply:** Present decisions as AskUserQuestion multiple choice with a recommended option and short trade-offs. Keep "Other" available for their own wording. Still push back in the option descriptions rather than in open prompts.
